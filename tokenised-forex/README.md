# Tokenised Forex and the On-Chain Financial Attack Surface

**Author:** Vektasafe
**Category:** Financial Markets Research

---

## Summary

The traditional forex market processes approximately 7.5 trillion USD per day. Blockchain infrastructure is beginning to bring this on-chain -- through synthetic assets, collateralised stablecoins, and decentralised perpetual exchanges. This research covers how tokenised forex works, which protocols are building it, and the five attack classes specific to on-chain financial assets.

## Protocols Covered

- Synthetix -- synthetic forex via overcollateralised debt model
- GMX -- decentralised perpetual exchange on Arbitrum and Avalanche
- Gains Network (gTrade) -- synthetic leveraged forex on Polygon and Arbitrum
- Ondo Finance -- tokenised real-world financial assets
